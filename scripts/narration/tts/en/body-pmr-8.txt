[warm, calm] Just relax. Easy to say. But if you feel you don't really know how, this session is made for you.
The idea is simple. You tense a group of muscles for a few seconds, then you let go. By feeling the tension, and then its absence, your body learns the difference.

If an area is injured or sore, or if you get a cramp, simply skip that step and imagine the movement instead. And while you're tensing, keep breathing normally. Don't hold your breath.
