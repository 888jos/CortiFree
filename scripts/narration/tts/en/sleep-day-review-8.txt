# sleep-day-review-8 · Close the day gently (EN)

[pause 2s]
[soft, unhurried] Settle in the way you like to fall asleep. On your back, on your side, the duvet pulled up as far as you like.

[pause 2s]
For a few minutes, let's close this day together. The way you close a book, slipping a bookmark between the pages.
Not to judge it. Not to replay every move. Just to put it away before you sleep.
There's nothing to get right here. If you fall asleep along the way, that's perfect too.

[calm, storytelling] Now you're going to rewind your day. Like a film playing backwards, a little faster than usual.

If other difficult moments show up, they can go in the same box. There's room for all of them.

[warm, soft] And now, the gentlest part.
You're going to look for three good things in this day. Just three.
They don't need to be big. Often the best ones are tiny.
A patch of sunlight on the pavement. A message that made you smile. A song on the radio. The first coffee, nice and hot.
Take your time. Let whatever comes, come, without hunting for the best answer.
