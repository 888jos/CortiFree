# morning-coffee-5 · The first coffee (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[warm, calm] You've got a cup in your hands, or you're about to pick one up. Coffee, tea, hot water, a plain glass of water... anything works.
For the next five minutes, this drink is your meditation. Nothing else to do but drink it, properly.
[pause 1.5s]
If your phone is in front of you, turn it face down, or move it a little further away. Your messages can wait for the length of one cup.
Sit down if you can. Feet on the floor, back supported.
[pause 2.5s]

[soft] Hold the cup in both hands. Wrap them all the way around it, fingers almost touching.
[almost a sigh] Let a first breath out, a long one, like a sigh that says, right, here I am.
[pause 4s]
[soft] And another, if you like. Your shoulders drop a little, hands still resting around the cup.
[pause 4s]
[calm] Feel the warmth in your palms. Where is it strongest?
[pause 2.5s]
Maybe in the hollow of your hand, maybe at your fingertips. Notice how it spreads, up into your wrists, sometimes as far as your forearms.
[pause 2s]
Notice the weight of the cup, too. Its texture. Smooth, a little rough, a tiny flaw in the glaze, the curve of the handle.
These are things you touch every single day, without ever really feeling them.
[pause 3s]

[gentle] Now bring the cup up toward your face, without drinking just yet.
Feel the steam on your skin, under your nose, on your lips.
[pause 2s]
[slow] Breathe in slowly through your nose. Let the aroma in.
No need to fill your lungs. A slow, light breath is enough for the scent to arrive.
[pause 3s]
[calm] What can you smell? Something roasted, toasty, maybe a little chocolatey. Or something floral and grassy, if it's tea. And if it's water, almost nothing... and that almost nothing is something you can notice too.
[pause 2s]
Breathe in again, more softly. Sometimes a second note appears, quieter than the first.
[pause 3s]
Notice what's happening in your mouth as well. Saliva gathering, your body getting ready. It already knows what's coming.
[pause 2s]

[soft, unhurried] Now, the first sip. A small one. Hold it in your mouth for a moment before you swallow.
Feel the temperature first. Hot, scalding, lukewarm.
Then the taste. Where does it land first? The tip of your tongue, the sides, the back?
[pause 2.5s]
Bitter, sweet, sharp, smooth... You don't need the right word. Just taste.
[pause 2s]
And when you're ready, swallow. Follow the path it takes. Down your throat, then that warmth sliding behind your breastbone, all the way to your stomach.
[pause 2s]
[warm] That small bit of attention changes everything. The same sip, gulped down over email, leaves no trace at all.
Stay a moment with the aftertaste. Whatever is left on your tongue once the sip is gone.
[pause 3s]

[calm] Put the cup down, or keep it in your hands, and look up.
If there's a window, look outside. The sky, its exact color this morning. A rooftop, a tree, a building across the street, someone walking past.
If there's no window, look at the light in the room. Where it falls, what it touches.
No need to think about what you see. Just look, as if for the very first time.
[pause 4s]

[soft] Second sip. Is it different from the first? It often is. A little less hot, the flavor a little more present.
[pause 2s]
[gentle] And maybe, somewhere in the background, part of you is getting impatient.
An urge to check the time. To run through today's list. To finish quickly and get on with things.
That's completely normal. In the morning, the mind loves to get ahead of itself.
Just notice that urge to hurry. You can even say to it, silently, I see you.
Then come back to the cup. To the warmth in your hands. That's all.
[pause 3s]

[unhurried] Take another sip or two, at your own pace. A sip, a glance outside. A sip, a breath.
Each time your mind races off toward the day ahead, the cup is right here to bring you back.
[pause 4s]
[warm] Notice how you feel now, compared to when you started. Maybe a little more settled. Maybe much the same. Both are fine.
[pause 2s]
What you just did, you can do again tomorrow. And the day after.
You have a drink every morning anyway. The moment is already part of your routine. All you need to add is your attention.
Even three mindful sips are a real practice. A way of starting the day by actually being in it, instead of already chasing after it.
[pause 1.5s]
[warm, slowing down] Finish your cup however you like. And when you get up, take a little of this calm with you.
[pause 4s]
