# sos-one-minute-1 · One minute to breathe (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[calm, clear] Stop for a moment. Right where you are, just as you are.
Standing, sitting, in a hallway... anywhere works. You can keep your eyes open.
[pause 1.5s]
[soft] Breathe in gently through your nose...
[pause 4s]
[slow] And let the air out even more slowly, as if through a straw...
[pause 6s]
[clear] Now, let's take a quick look inside. Three questions, one word each.
Your body. What stands out? Tight, hot, tired... Just one word.
[pause 2.5s]
Your emotion. Irritation, worry, nothing much? One word, no judgment.
[pause 2.5s]
Your mind. What is it busy with, right now? One more word.
[pause 3s]
[warm] Simply naming it already gives you a little distance.
[soft] One more slow breath. In...
[pause 4s]
[slow] And out, no rush, all the way to the end.
[pause 6s]
[calm, grounded] Now, move on. What's the next small thing to do? Not the whole day. Just the very next step.
Picking up that call. Opening that door. Answering that one message.
[pause 2s]
[warm, encouraging] You have it? Then go, with this calmer breath.
[pause 4s]
