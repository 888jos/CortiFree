[calm] Waking up is a transition. If you jump straight into a screen, your body switches to alert mode before you're even out of bed. This morning, we'll take the slower road.

[warm] And now, stretch. Like a cat getting up from a nap. There's no set order, and no right way.

[soft, unhurried] Now let everything go. Settle onto your back, or onto your side, whichever you prefer.
