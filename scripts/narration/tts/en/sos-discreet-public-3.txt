# sos-discreet-public-3 · Stressed in public, discreetly (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[calm, reassuring] No one around you will notice a thing. Keep your eyes open and stay just as you are.
Everything we'll do is invisible. From the outside, you simply look like someone waiting.
[pause 2s]
[clear, grounded] Let's start with your feet. Standing or sitting, press them lightly into the floor, as if you wanted to push it down by a millimeter.
Feel the pressure under your heels, then under the balls of your feet. The ground pushes back. It's holding you up.
[pause 1.5s]
Release... and press again, gently.
That simple contact reminds your body that it's standing somewhere real, here and now.
[pause 3s]
[calm] Now your hands. They can stay in your pockets, on your lap, or around a rail.
Press the tip of your thumb against your index finger. A small, firm squeeze, for a couple of seconds.
Then against your middle finger. Your ring finger. Your little finger.
[pause 1.5s]
And back the other way, feeling each contact. The warmth, the texture of the skin, the fingernail under your thumb.
If your thoughts are racing, this gives them a rhythm to follow. One finger, then the next.
[pause 2s]
[soft] Let your gaze rest on something neutral. A coat button, the corner of a poster, a mark on the floor.
No need to stare. Let your eyes settle there, the way they do when you look out of a train window.
The edges of what you see can widen a little. Your brow softens.
[pause 3s]
[calm, unhurried] Now the breath. Your mouth stays closed, so no one hears a thing.
Breathe in easily through your nose, without lifting your chest...
[pause 3s]
[slow, soothing] and let the breath out last a little longer. The air slides out through your nostrils, slowly, silently...
[pause 5s]
Once more. An ordinary breath in...
[pause 3s]
and a breath out that takes its time.
[pause 5s]
[soft] At the end of the exhale, your shoulders drop by half an inch. Invisible to everyone else, but not to you.
A longer exhale gently tells your body it can slow down.
[pause 2s]
[calm] Keep this quiet rhythm and listen to what's around you.
Pick out a first sound. The hum of an engine, a distant voice, someone typing.
Don't judge it. Just notice it.
[pause 2s]
Then a second one, closer. Footsteps, the rustle of a jacket.
[pause 2s]
And a third, quieter one. The kind you only hear when you really listen.
[pause 3s]
[warm, grounded] Three sounds, and here you are, in this place, rather than inside a busy head.
The noise and the crowd haven't changed. But you've found your anchors again. Your feet, your fingers, your gaze, your breath.
[pause 2s]
[warm, calm] You can do this anywhere. In a line, in a meeting, in a packed elevator.
Even thirty seconds can help. A press of the feet, a round of the fingers, one long exhale.
[pause 1.5s]
[soft, warm, slowing down] Go back to what you were doing, at your own pace. Your anchor travels with you.
[pause 4s]
