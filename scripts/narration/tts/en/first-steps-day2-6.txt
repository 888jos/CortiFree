[pause 1.5s]
Because the heart of meditation isn't staying perfectly focused. It's the moment you realize your mind has drifted, and you bring it back.
That move has three steps. Let's learn them together.
[pause 1.5s]
[calm] The first step is to notice. At some point, you realize, oh, I was thinking. That little click is already a moment of presence.
The second step is to let go. You don't need to finish the thought, or push it away. You just set it down, the way you'd put an object on a table.
The third step is to come back. You gently bring your attention back to your belly, to the breath that's happening right now.
[pause 1.5s]
Notice, let go, come back. No judging, no sighing at yourself.
[warm] The tone really matters. Picture a puppy wandering off down the sidewalk. You don't scold it. You just bring it back, kindly, as many times as it takes.
