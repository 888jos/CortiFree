[warm, calm] This practice is very old. It's called metta, or loving-kindness. It means offering wishes of well-being, first to yourself, then to wider and wider circles of people.
It isn't a way of forcing a feeling. You can't produce love on demand. You're simply watering a seed, and letting it grow at its own pace.
Research suggests that practicing this regularly may help people feel more connected to others, and a little gentler with themselves. Two precious things when stress pulls us into isolation.
