# body-quick-scan-3 · Quick body scan (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[warm, calm] Three minutes, just for you. Wherever you are, in your chair or standing up, is perfectly fine.
Close your eyes if you'd like, or let your gaze rest low, on the desk or the floor in front of you.
[pause 2s]
[soft] Breathe in through your nose...
[pause 4s]
[almost a sigh] and breathe out a little longer, like a quiet sigh.
[pause 5s]
[calm, unhurried] Let's take a quick tour of the body, the way you'd pat your pockets before heading out.
Start with your feet. Feel them against the floor, the weight in your heels.
Your toes can soften inside your shoes.
[pause 2s]
Your calves, your knees, your thighs. Let them be heavy, simply resting, as if your legs had nothing left to carry.
Now your hips. Feel how your pelvis sits on the chair, the weight settling there, the seat holding you up.
[pause 2s]
Then your lower back. If you're slumped, lift up just a little, without stiffening.
All along your back, let the chair, or your own spine, do the holding. You don't have to hold yourself up alone.
[pause 2s]
[gentle] Now your belly. Is it pulled in, tight?
[pause 2s]
You can let it go, let it be soft, rounding a little as you breathe in.
Your chest. Notice your breath lifting it, without trying to change anything. Just this movement, rising and falling.
[pause 3s]
[soft] Your shoulders. They often creep up without us noticing, especially in front of a screen.
Let them drop half an inch, and then a little more, as if someone had rested warm hands on them.
Your arms grow heavy, from your shoulders down to your elbows, from your elbows down to your wrists.
Your hands rest, your fingers uncurl. There's nothing you need to hold on to right now.
[pause 2s]
[softer] Your face. Your forehead smooths out, the space between your eyebrows widens. Your cheeks soften.
Your eyes rest behind your eyelids. And your jaw... are your teeth touching? Let a little space open between them, lips gently closed, tongue at ease.
[pause 3s]
[slow, unhurried] And now your whole body at once, from your feet to the top of your head. One single presence, breathing.
[pause 3s]
[warm] Think of the spot that felt tightest a moment ago. Has it shifted at all? If not, that's okay. You noticed it, and that already counts for a lot.
[pause 2s]
[soft] Let's take one last breath together. Breathe in slowly through your nose, feeling your whole body...
[pause 4s]
[slow, almost a sigh] And breathe out through your mouth, long and slow, letting go of whatever is ready to go.
[pause 6s]
[warm, clear] Open your eyes if they were closed, or lift your gaze. Wiggle your fingers and toes, and roll your shoulders once.
[warm, slowing down] You can come back to this little tour any time today, even for thirty seconds, between two emails.
[pause 4s]
