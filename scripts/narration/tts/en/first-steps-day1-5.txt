# first-steps-day1-5 · Day 1 · Sit and breathe (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[warm, calm] Hello, and welcome to your very first day.
If you've never meditated before, you're in exactly the right place.
For the next five minutes, there's nothing to get right. No score, no right or wrong way to do this.
Your only job is to be here. And you already are.
[pause 2s]
[warm, clear] Before we start, let's clear up a very common misunderstanding.
A lot of people think meditating means emptying your mind. Not thinking about anything at all.
If that were true, almost nobody could do it.
Meditation is really about noticing. Noticing what's happening inside you and around you, while it's happening.
Thoughts will keep coming. That's normal. It's simply what your brain does all day long.
You're just going to learn to watch them pass, and to come back to something very simple. Your breath.
[pause 2s]
[soft, unhurried] Let's get settled.
Sit on a chair, on your sofa, or on a cushion, wherever feels comfortable.
If you're on a chair, place both feet flat on the floor, roughly hip-width apart.
Let your back lengthen, as if a thread were gently drawing the top of your head toward the ceiling.
Upright, but not stiff. You're not standing to attention. You're just here, steady and at ease.
[pause 1.5s]
Let your shoulders drop a little, away from your ears.
Rest your hands on your thighs or your knees, palms up or palms down, whichever you prefer.
Now you can close your eyes.
If you'd rather keep them open, that's perfectly fine. Just let your gaze rest on the floor, about a meter ahead, without focusing on anything.
[pause 3s]
[calm] To really arrive, we'll take three breaths that are a little deeper than usual.
Nothing forced. Just a bit more air, and a bit more slowness.
[soft] Breathe in through your nose, unhurried, letting your belly rise...
[pause 3s]
[slow, almost a sigh] and breathe out through your mouth, slowly, like a long sigh.
[pause 5s]
[soft] Once more. Breathe in, and feel your chest open a little...
[pause 3s]
and breathe out, letting your shoulders soften as the air leaves.
[pause 5s]
One last time. Breathe in gently...
[pause 3s]
and let it all go.
[pause 5s]
[calm, unhurried] Now let your breathing find its own rhythm. You don't need to steer it anymore. It has always known how to breathe on its own.
Your job is simply to watch it.
Where do you feel your breath most clearly?
[pause 2s]
Maybe at the edge of your nostrils, where the air feels a little cooler coming in, and a little warmer going out.
Maybe in your chest, rising and falling.
Or in your belly, gently swelling as you breathe in, and softening as you breathe out.
There's no right place. Just choose wherever it feels clearest for you today, and stay there.
[pause 2s]
[soft] Feel the very beginning of the in-breath, the moment the air arrives.
Then the tiny moment at the top, where everything pauses.
And the out-breath, flowing away, with no effort at all.
[pause 3s]
[calm, intimate] For the next minute, I'll speak softly, and not very much. You'll simply follow your breath, one breath after another.
If a thought shows up, and it will, that's not a problem. You notice it, and you come back to the breath.
That's all there is to it.
[pause 4s]
[soft] One breath in. One breath out. You don't need to count them, or make them perfect.
You might notice they don't all last the same. Often, the air takes a little longer to leave than it does to arrive.
[pause 4s]
[gentle, reassuring] If your mind has wandered off, you've just noticed it. That's exactly what meditating is. Come back, without judging yourself.
[pause 3s]
[soft] Just this breath. Not the last one, not the next one.
If your body needs to move a little, go ahead, slowly, and then find your breath again.
[pause 4s]
A few more breaths, at your own pace. The air coming in. The air going out.
[pause 5s]
[warm, calm] Gently, let your attention widen. Feel the weight of your body on your seat again, your feet on the floor.
[pause 2s]
What you've just done, over these few minutes, is meditate. Not a failed attempt, not a rough draft. Real meditation.
You sat down, you watched your breath, your mind may have wandered, and you found your way back.
Everything else in this course builds on these simple steps.
[pause 2s]
[warm, clear] Whenever you're ready, open your eyes, or slowly lift your gaze.
During your day, you can come back to one conscious breath, anywhere at all. Just one is enough to bring you back here.
[soft, warm, slowing down] Thank you for taking this time for yourself. See you tomorrow, for day two.
[pause 4s]
