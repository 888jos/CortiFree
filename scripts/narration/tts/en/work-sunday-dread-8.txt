[clear] Before you close the notebook, choose one preparatory action. Just one, simple and concrete, something you can do tonight in under five minutes.
Lay out your clothes for tomorrow. Put your badge in your bag. Set your alarm. Write down Monday's first task.
Or nothing at all, if everything's ready.
Decide on it now. You'll do it right after this session, and then it's done.
The goal isn't to prepare the whole week. It's to show your mind you've got it handled, just enough so it can rest.
Small, concrete steps like this often calm the mind more than hours of going over the week in your head.
