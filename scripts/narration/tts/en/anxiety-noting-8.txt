[calm, clear] Your breath will be your point of return, your home base. Every time you drift away from it, you can come back.
Here's how mental noting works. When something pulls your attention away from the breath, you notice it, and you give it a word. Just one, simple and soft.
A door closing, for example, is hearing. An itch is sensation. A thought about your to-do list is planning.
Then, without lingering, you come back to the breath.
Think of it as a light tap on the shoulder. You notice, you name, and you let go.

One word every few seconds is plenty. This isn't a labeling race. Between two notes, there's the breath, calm, carrying on.

[gentle] Many people find that naming what they feel helps them step back a little. You're no longer inside the thought. You're watching it pass.
