[calm, storytelling] First, an idea that often surprises people. Stress, in itself, isn't the problem.
Your body is built to rise to a challenge, and then come back down. That's how it's meant to work.
What wears us down, many researchers say, is when the coming down never happens. When we go from effort to effort without ever recovering.
[pause 1.5s]
Picture a rubber band. If you stretch it and let it go often, it stays supple for years.
Keep it stretched all day, though, and it tires and loses its spring. Your nervous system is a little like that rubber band.
Many of us wait for the evening, or the weekend, to finally let go. By then, the fatigue has piled up.
Studies on work suggest that short, regular breaks, even a few minutes long, can help you keep your energy and limit fatigue through the day.
