# body-standing-shake-5 · Shake off stress (EN)
[pause 1.5s]
[warm, calm] Sometimes, sitting still and breathing calmly just isn't possible. Your body is too wound up, and it needs to move. So let's move.
[pause 1.5s]
[clear] Stand up, if you can. Feet hip-width apart, knees soft and slightly bent, arms hanging by your sides.
[gentle, reassuring] One rule for the whole session. Stay within what's comfortable. No jumping, no jerky movements, never any pain. If your back or knees are sore, do all of this sitting down, and shake just your arms and shoulders. That works too.
[pause 2s]
[calm] Breathe in through your nose...
[pause 3s]
and blow out through your mouth, all the way.
[pause 3s]
[clear, encouraging] Let's start with your hands. Let them hang, wrists loose, and shake them. As if your fingers were wet and there was no towel in sight.
Fingers completely relaxed, flapping about. Go a little faster, or a little slower. Find what feels good.
[pause 2s]
Now let the movement travel up into your wrists, your forearms, then your elbows.
Your whole arms are shaking now, floppy as ropes. In front of you, out to the sides, down low. Change direction whenever you like.
Do your shoulders want to join in?
[pause 2s]
Let them. They can bounce, shrug and drop.
[pause 3s]
[clear] Now the legs. Without leaving the floor, start bouncing gently on your heels. They lift just slightly and drop, again and again.
It's a small, springy bounce that comes from the knees. Feel the vibration travel up through your calves, your thighs, into your hips.
[pause 1.5s]
If you like, lift one foot and shake that leg, as if you were shaking sand out of your shoe. Then the other leg. Hold on to a wall or a chair if you need a little balance.
Come back onto both feet and keep bouncing. Let your breath stay free, through your nose or your mouth, however it comes.
[pause 2s]
[encouraging] Now let your whole body join in. Heels bouncing, arms shaking, shoulders moving, belly jiggling too.
Let your head follow along, without throwing it around. Just loose, carried by everything else.
And your jaw... let it hang. Mouth slightly open, cheeks soft and wobbling a little. It might feel a bit silly, and that's perfectly fine.
If a sound wants to come out, let it. A loud breath, a long sigh, a grunt, a laugh. Nobody's grading you.
[pause 1.5s]
[warm] Your body has built up momentum to face a danger that isn't coming. You're simply giving it a way to spend that energy.
Keep going at your own pace. Shake out whatever needs shaking. Your upper back, your hips, your hands again, wherever things feel stuck.
[pause 3s]
A few more seconds. Put a little more energy into it, if that feels good. One last burst, as if you were shaking off the last crumbs of the day.
[pause 2s]
[calm, slowing down] And now, start to slow down. No sudden stop. The movement gets smaller, slower.
Your arms swing gently. Your heels barely bounce. Like a merry-go-round coming to the end of its ride.
Your shoulders settle, your knees soften, your hands slow down little by little. Smaller still. Just a slight tremble in your hands, in your knees.
[slow, quieter] And then... still.
[pause 3s]
[soft, unhurried] Stay standing, knees still a little bent, arms hanging. Close your eyes if you feel steady, otherwise rest your gaze on the floor in front of you.
What's happening in your hands right now?
[pause 2s]
Maybe a tingling, a buzzing, a sense of aliveness under the skin.
Maybe warmth in your arms, in your face. Your heart beating a little faster, and starting to slow. You might feel it in your chest, or in your fingertips.
There's nothing to do with these sensations. Just watch them, the way you'd watch bubbles rising to the surface.
[pause 3s]
[grounded] Feel your feet flat on the floor. Your weight sinking into the ground, spread between your heels and the balls of your feet.
Let your breath settle. No need to control it. Just notice how it finds its own way back toward a calmer rhythm.
[pause 2s]
[slow, soothing] Breathe in through your nose...
[pause 3s]
and let the breath out last a little longer, like a wave drawing back from the shore.
[pause 5s]
Once more. Breathe in...
[pause 3s]
and out, slowly.
[pause 5s]
[warm, gentle] Notice the contrast between the commotion a moment ago and this calm. Your body found it all by itself, once that energy had somewhere to go.
[pause 2s]
[warm, calm] Open your eyes if they were closed. Take a few steps, look around you.
[soft, warm, slowing down] Next time you feel wired, remember this. You don't have to sit still to calm down. You can shake first, and then stop.
