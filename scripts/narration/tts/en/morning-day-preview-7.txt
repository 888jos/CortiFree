It's not about worrying ahead of time. Quite the opposite. A moment you've already looked at squarely is often less scary than one you avoid imagining.

Maybe there's a tense moment. A comment, a tricky question, something unexpected. See yourself taking it in, pausing briefly, and answering at your own pace.
You don't have to be flawless in this scene. Just present and calm, in your own way.
If your mind heads for the worst, gently steer the scene back to a simple, realistic version. Not perfect, not a disaster.

[clear] Now let's turn that into a simple plan, in two parts. If this happens, then I do that.

The second part is your response. Something tiny and concrete. Then I plant my feet and breathe out slowly. Then I take a sip of water before answering. Then I start with one task, the smallest one.
Choose a response you can actually do on the spot, discreetly, even with other people around. A breath, a gesture, a phrase you say to yourself.
